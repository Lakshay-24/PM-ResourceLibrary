VendorFlow

A deterministic, AI-assisted vendor onboarding workflow that turns a messy operational intake into an explainable decision, explicit exception queue and audit trail. The prototype is intentionally transparent: rules decide the outcome; AI would assist document extraction and exception summarization, not silently approve vendors.

Design choices

Automation with guardrails

Low-risk, complete vendors can pass automatically. Material risk hits are rejected. Ambiguous or incomplete cases route to a human with precise reason codes.

AI where it helps

Use document extraction to populate fields and an LLM to summarize exceptions. Keep policy checks deterministic and logged so operators can explain every decision.

Measure business impact

Track straight-through rate, median onboarding time, review backlog, false-positive overrides, resubmission rate and post-onboarding exceptions.
